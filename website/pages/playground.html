The real OxigenLang interpreter, compiled to WebAssembly, runs in your browser with files in memory: io, os, input(), time.sleep, <test> blocks, and your own modules in other tabs. Not available in a browser: the network, running programs, and C libraries. Runs stop after 20 seconds of computing.
Ctrl+Enter runs, Ctrl+S formats, Ctrl+Space completes, Ctrl+/ comments lines out. In vim keys, :w formats, :run and :test run. Esc then Tab leaves the editor. Syntax reference · Install Oxigen
